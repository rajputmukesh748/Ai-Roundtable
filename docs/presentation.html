The debate

It feels like a real conversation

  • Anyone can jump in. The other side is more likely to answer.
  • Short replies: one or two sentences, one new point.
  • Each sentence is spoken as it appears, with male and female voices.
  • A pause after every message so you can follow along.

The judge

A scorecard, not a guess

Argument8 vs 6
Evidence4 vs 2
Rebuttal3 vs 1
Clarity9 vs 7
For 60%
Against 40%
  • Scores each side 0 to 10 on argument, evidence, rebuttal and clarity.
  • The app works out the winner from the scores.
  • 53% or more wins; 47 to 53% is a draw.
  • Gives a short reason and each side's key points.

Data and privacy

You decide where your data goes

🏠

Local by default

The app and voices always run on your machine. With a local model, nothing goes to an outside service. With an online model, the debate text goes to that provider.

🗂️

Saved for later

Topic, debaters, every message and the verdict are stored so any debate can be reopened or deleted. API keys stay in your local database and are never shown again in the browser.
